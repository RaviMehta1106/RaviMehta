Projects:

Handheld Taser

This project is one of my simpler ones, I had the desire to make a taser because it seemed fun and I wanted to get some practice with soldering. The materials I used consisted of a 9V battery and connector, a simple button, two nails which were cut in half, and a boost converter. Everything was wired up and soldered to make good electrical connections. The boost converter was the most important part, triggering a switch rapidly to take advantage of the properties of capacitors and inductors to exponentially increase the voltage output. Each time the current is interrupted, the inductor’s collapsing magnetic field pushes energy forward through a diode into a capacitor, and the voltage there builds up higher than the input.
