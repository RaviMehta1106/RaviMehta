Real Stuff

Part of the reason I wanted to make this website was to relive the golden days of the internet which I missed on account of me being born in 2006. The idea of people personalizing their mySpace pages to express themselves in the same way you might decorate your bedroom always sounded like the coolest thing ever to me.

Since you might not know anything about me, I'll take the liberty of doing a few ice breakers. I'm a dual Swiss citizen, I can walk on my hands, I play the drums in a band called "Azmatix", I love Haribo gummy bears, and my favorite co-op game is Halo 2. My hottest take is that I think Jared Leto was not a bad Joker in Suicide Squad. I get flack for this, but I think a tweaked-out, uncharismatic, weirdo with bad tattoos is exactly what the Joker would actually be like.
